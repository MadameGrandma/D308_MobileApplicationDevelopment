# Welcome to D308 Vacation Planner!

### This application allows users to plan out their vacations by entering custom details such as:
| Vacations:      | Excursions:         |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Title           | Title               |
| Accommodations  | Dates               |
| Start Dates     | Notes               |
| End Dates       | Assign to Vacations |

 Within those items, you can also delete existing vacations and excursions, share details of them with your friends, and set notifications to let you know key vacation dates!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### How To Use
#### Below I'm going to walk you through the basics on how to use our app!
1. Enter the main screen to start on your vacation list.
   1. This will contain all the titles of your saved vacations. If it's empty, nothing is saved yet (Don't panic!)
2. To enter a new vacation, click the + icon in the bottom right. This will bring you to our details page.
3. Here's your vacation details page! Before you enter your details, note a couple things
   1. Every field is required, no skipping
   2. Make sure your title is recognizable! This will be important once we start creating excursions. Think "My Awesome Birthday Trip to Bali".
   3. Dates must be entered in "MM/dd/yyyy" format _(Americans, am I right?)_
   4. If plans change, you can always come back and update this vacation later
4. Ready to save? Click the triple dots in the top right to open your options menu. Here you can:
   1. Save your vacation
   2. Delete existing vacations
   3. Share your vacation details
   4. Set notifications for the start and end dates of your trip
5. Go ahead and save your vacation. Once it has saved successfully, you'll be brought back to the main Vacation List page.
6. Reopen our new vacation by selecting it's name in the menu. Now we're going to add an excursion!
   1. At first the area under your details will be blank, but as we add excursions they'll show up here for you to select.
7. On vacation details, click the + in the bottom right corner
8. Here's your excursion details page! Before you enter your details, note a couple things
   1. Your excursion date must happen during the dates of the matching vacation
   2. Dates must be entered in "MM/dd/yyyy" format
   3. The drop down menu will show you all of the vacations you've saved. Make sure to select which vacation you want to associate this excursion with, otherwise it will be saved to the first vacation all of the time
   4. If plans change, you can always come back and update this excursion later
9. Ready to save? Click the triple dots in the top right to open your options menu. Here you can:
   1. Save your excursion
   2. Delete existing excursions
   3. Share your excursion details
   4. Set notifications for the date  of your excursion
10. Go ahead and save your vacation
11. **_Pat yourself on the back because you just planned your first trip!_**
